# Dan Xu

PhD student in Economics at Rutgers University, with intended fields in macro-finance and financial econometrics. Previously at USC, Brown and Boston University.

Research at the intersection of empirical macroeconomics, financial market microstructure, and comparative political economy.

## Working Papers

- **[Learning to Price Political Risk](https://github.com/danxudanxu/learning-to-price-political-risk)** — High-frequency event study of US-China trade policy effects on equity volatility across two Trump administrations. NYSE TAQ Millisecond + RESSET intraday data.
- **[Pushing on a String through the ZLB Decade](https://github.com/danxudanxu/pushing-on-a-string-zlb)** — State-dependent local projections replication and 17-year extension of Tenreyro and Thwaites (2016, *AEJ:Macro*).
